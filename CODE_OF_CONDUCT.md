# Community conduct

Participants should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss ideas
and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and
sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately to Gavin L.
Simpson at ucfagls@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed with care and shared only
as needed to address the concern. This applies to project issues, pull requests,
and other project-related interactions.
